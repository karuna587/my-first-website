Dr. S.R. Ranganathan

Father of Library Science in India

Dr. S.R. Ranganathan became the first University Librarian at Madras in 1924, a pivotal role that shifted his focus from mathematics to modern information management. The university subsequently sponsored his research travel to London, inspiring him to reform traditional library structures upon his return.

About University Library

The Mother Library of South India

The Madras University Library, established in 1907, is often called the "mother library" of all academic libraries in South India. It is housed in a magnificent heritage building built in the Indo-British architectural style.
